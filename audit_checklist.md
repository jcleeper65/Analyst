# Audit checklist

| # | Claim | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Total revenue, latest fiscal year | income statement; the revenue tag for that period |
| 2 | Revenue growth versus the prior year | computed from the two years in the same filing |
| 3 | Operating income and operating margin | income statement; margin = operating income ÷ total revenue |
| 4 | Net income attributable to the company | income statement |
| 5 | Diluted earnings per share, GAAP | per-share section |
| 6 | Return on average equity | net income ÷ the average of this year's and last year's stockholders' equity, balance sheet |
| 7 | Debt to equity | long-term debt including its current portion ÷ stockholders' equity, balance sheet |
| 8 | Free cash flow | cash from operations − additions to property and equipment, cash flow statement |
| 9 | The latest quarter's revenue | the earnings release, income statement |
| 10 | The quarter's growth versus the same quarter last year | computed from the release's two columns |
| 11 | The quarter's diluted EPS, GAAP | the release, per-share section |
