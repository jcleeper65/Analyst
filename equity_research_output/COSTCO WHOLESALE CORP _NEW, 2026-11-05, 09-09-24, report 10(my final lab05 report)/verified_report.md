## Overview
Costco runs membership warehouses and e-commerce sites on a low-price, high-volume model. The 10-K says the concept is that "offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally-branded and private-label products in a wide range of categories will produce high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover" [10-K.txt:L180]. The footprint is 939 warehouses, up from 914 a year earlier: "Costco operated 939, 914, and 890 warehouses worldwide at August 30, 2026, August 31, 2025, and September 1, 2024." [10-K.txt:L177]

Fiscal 2026 results (52 weeks ended August 30, 2026) are consistent with management's growth claim: "Net sales increased 10% to $297,247, driven by an increase in comparable sales and sales at new warehouses;" [10-K.txt:L709] and "Net income increased 14% to $9,226, $20.76 per diluted share, compared to $8,099, $18.21 per diluted share in 2025; and" [10-K.txt:L721].

Revenue concentration is the main structural exposure. "Our financial and operational performance is highly dependent on our U.S. and Canadian operations, which comprised 86% and 83% of net sales and operating income in 2026." [10-K.txt:L350] Within the U.S., "our California operations, which comprised 26% of U.S. net sales in 2026" [10-K.txt:L350]. Supplier concentration is not a stated issue: "We do not obtain a significant portion of merchandise from any one supplier." [10-K.txt:L211] Gasoline is a notable sales component: "Our gasoline business represented 11% of total net sales in 2026." [10-K.txt:L207]
