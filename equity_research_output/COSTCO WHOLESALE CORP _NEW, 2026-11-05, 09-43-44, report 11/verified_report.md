## Overview
Costco is a membership-warehouse retailer. It describes its model as: "We operate membership warehouses and e-commerce sites based on the concept that offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally-branded and private-label products in a wide range of categories will produce high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover." [10-K.txt:L180] The warehouse base was "939, 914, and 890 warehouses worldwide at August 30, 2026, August 31, 2025, and September 1, 2024." [10-K.txt:L177] Management says the model depends on limited selection: "We carry less than 4,000 active stock keeping units (SKUs) per warehouse in our core warehouse business, significantly less than other broadline retailers." [10-K.txt:L187]

The reported scale for fiscal 2026 (52 weeks ended August 30, 2026) is "Net sales increased 10% to $297,247, driven by an increase in comparable sales and sales at new warehouses;" [10-K.txt:L709] and "Net income increased 14% to $9,226, $20.76 per diluted share, compared to $8,099, $18.21 per diluted share in 2025; and" [10-K.txt:L721]. The earnings release says "Net sales for the quarter increased 11.2 percent, to $93.9 billion, from $84.4 billion last year." [earnings_release_Ex99-1.txt:L10]

I treat management's growth as a claim to test. Three features of the business shape that test.
- Revenue concentration is high: "Our financial and operational performance is highly dependent on our U.S. and Canadian operations, which comprised 86% and 83% of net sales and operating income in 2026." [10-K.txt:L350] Within the U.S., "we are highly dependent on our California operations, which comprised 26% of U.S. net sales in 2026." [10-K.txt:L350]
- International is smaller but more profitable per sales dollar: "During 2026, our international operations, including Canada, generated 27% and 35% of our net sales and operating income." [10-K.txt:L407]
- Supplier concentration is not a stated issue: "We do not obtain a significant portion of merchandise from any one supplier." [10-K.txt:L211]

Gasoline adds sales but dilutes margin. "Our gasoline business represented 11% of total net sales in 2026." [10-K.txt:L207] "A higher penetration of gasoline sales will generally lower our gross margin percentage." [10-K.txt:L693]
